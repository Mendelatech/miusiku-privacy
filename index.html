Privacy Policy — Miusiku

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Miusiku is a local music player for Android. This page explains what data the app does and does not handle.

No account, no personal data collection

Miusiku does not require you to create an account or sign in. The app does not collect, sell, or share your personal information with any third party.

Your music stays on your device

Your music library and playlists are read from and stored only on your device, using local storage. Nothing about your library or playlists is uploaded to any server.

Lyrics lookup (the only network request the app makes)

To show synced lyrics, Miusiku sends the song title and artist name to the LRCLIB (lrclib.net) service over a secure (HTTPS) connection. This is the only data Miusiku sends over the network, and it is only sent when you view a song's lyrics.

Permissions used

  • Audio files access — to find and play the music already on your device.
  • Notifications — to show playback controls while music is playing.
  • Internet — to fetch lyrics, as described above.

No analytics, advertising, or tracking

Miusiku does not use analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or tracking services of any kind.
