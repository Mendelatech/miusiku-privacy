Privacy Policy — Miusiku

Last updated: August 24, 2026

No account, no personal data collection

Miusiku is a local music player. It does not require an account, and it does not collect, sell, or share your personal information.

Your music stays on your device

Your music library and playlists are read from and stored only on your device — nothing is uploaded to any server.

Lyrics lookup (the only network request the app makes)

To show synced lyrics, Miusiku sends the song title and artist name to the LRCLIB (lrclib.net) service over a secure (HTTPS) connection. This is the only data sent over the network.

Permissions used

Miusiku uses the following device permissions: access to your audio files (to build your library), notifications (to show playback controls while music is playing), and network access (to fetch lyrics). No analytics, advertising, or tracking services are used.
